# Medical Research — AI-Driven Biochemical Discovery

> Exploring the intersection of biochemical processes and artificial intelligence to accelerate medical breakthroughs.

## Mission

We investigate fundamental biochemical pathways and leverage modern AI/ML techniques to uncover novel therapeutic approaches, drug targets, and diagnostic markers. Our goal is to bridge the gap between computational biology and clinical medicine.

## Research Areas

- **Protein Folding & Structure Prediction** — Using deep learning to predict protein conformations and their implications for drug design
- **Metabolic Pathway Analysis** — ML-driven modeling of metabolic networks to identify dysregulation in disease states
- **Drug-Target Interaction Prediction** — Graph neural networks for predicting molecular binding affinities
- **Genomic Biomarker Discovery** — Transformer-based models for identifying clinically relevant genetic variants
- **Clinical Trial Optimization** — Reinforcement learning approaches for adaptive trial design

## Tech Stack

- Python, PyTorch, JAX
- AlphaFold / ESMFold pipelines
- RDKit, DeepChem for molecular modeling
- Hugging Face Transformers for biomedical NLP
- MLflow for experiment tracking
