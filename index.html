Research Focus

Bridging Biochemistry & AI

Our interdisciplinary approach targets key biochemical processes where machine learning can accelerate discovery and improve clinical outcomes.

🧪

Protein Structure & Drug Design

Leveraging deep learning models like AlphaFold and ESMFold to predict protein conformations. We analyze binding pockets and molecular interactions to identify novel drug targets for autoimmune diseases, cancer, and neurodegenerative disorders.

AlphaFold Molecular Dynamics Drug Targets
🔬

Metabolic Pathway Analysis

Using graph neural networks and systems biology approaches to model complex metabolic networks. We identify pathway dysregulation patterns in disease states to find metabolic biomarkers and therapeutic intervention points.

Graph Neural Networks Systems Biology Metabolomics
💊

Drug-Target Interaction Prediction

Applying attention-based GNNs and transformer architectures to predict molecular binding affinities with high accuracy. Our models screen millions of compounds against target proteins, reducing wet-lab validation cycles by orders of magnitude.

Transformers Binding Affinity Virtual Screening
🧬

Genomic Biomarker Discovery

Training large language models on genomic sequences to identify clinically relevant genetic variants and expression signatures. We focus on rare diseases and oncology, where traditional GWAS approaches often fall short.

Genomic LLMs Variant Calling Precision Medicine
📊

Clinical Trial Optimization

Developing reinforcement learning frameworks for adaptive clinical trial design. Our approach dynamically adjusts patient allocation, dosing, and endpoints based on interim data, improving statistical power and reducing time-to-approval.

Reinforcement Learning Adaptive Design Bayesian Methods
🏥

Medical Imaging & Diagnostics

Combining biochemical markers with medical imaging through multi-modal AI models. We develop diagnostic pipelines that integrate histopathology, radiology, and molecular data for more accurate disease classification and prognosis.

Multi-Modal AI Histopathology Diagnostic Pipeline
Methodology

Our Research Pipeline

A structured, reproducible approach from hypothesis to clinical validation.

01

Biochemical Hypothesis

Identify molecular targets and pathways with clinical relevance through literature mining and expert consultation.

02

Data Acquisition

Curate datasets from public databases (PDB, UniProt, TCGA, GEO) and proprietary clinical data partnerships.

03

Model Development

Design and train specialized ML architectures tailored to the biochemical domain and specific research question.

04

In-Silico Validation

Rigorous computational validation using cross-validation, ablation studies, and comparison against established baselines.

05

Wet-Lab Collaboration

Partner with laboratory teams to validate computational predictions through biochemical assays and cell-based experiments.

06

Clinical Translation

Work with medical professionals to translate findings into diagnostic tools, treatment protocols, or clinical trial designs.
