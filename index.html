Methodology

Research Pipeline

From hypothesis to clinical translation — a structured, reproducible approach.

PHASE 01

Biochemical Hypothesis

Identify molecular targets and pathways with clinical relevance through literature mining, knowledge graphs, and expert consultation.

PHASE 02

Data Curation

Aggregate from PDB, UniProt, TCGA, GEO, ChEMBL, and proprietary clinical partnerships. Quality-controlled, harmonized, ready for training.

PHASE 03

Architecture Design

Custom ML architectures tailored to the biochemical domain. Not off-the-shelf — purpose-built for molecular understanding.

PHASE 04

In-Silico Validation

Rigorous benchmarking: cross-validation, ablation studies, comparison against SOTA. We don't publish unless we beat the baseline.

PHASE 05

Wet-Lab Confirmation

Partner with laboratory teams for biochemical assays, cell-based experiments, and animal model validation of computational predictions.

PHASE 06

Clinical Translation

Transform validated findings into diagnostic tools, treatment protocols, or clinical trial designs — the final mile from bench to bedside.
